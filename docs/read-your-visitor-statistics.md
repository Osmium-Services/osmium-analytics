**Osmium Analytics** counts visits to your site without cookies. It records page views, where visitors came from, device type and country. IP addresses are never stored.

## The pages

- **Visits** shows a **Traffic Trend** of daily hits over the last 30 days, plus **Devices** and **Countries** by hits.
- **Top Pages** shows total hits, unique visitors, pages visited and today's hits, then a list of the most visited pages with their hits, unique visitors and average hits per day.
- **Top Referrers** shows which websites send you visitors, with the share of referral traffic and a daily trend. **Direct Traffic** counts visitors who arrived without a referrer.

## Settings

Open **Osmium Analytics Settings**.

- **Tracking enabled** switches counting on or off.
- **Ignore signed-in admins** stops your own visits from being counted.
- **Keep data for** sets how long records are kept. Older rows are deleted automatically, at most once a day. Choose **Forever** to keep everything.

## Country breakdown

Countries stay empty until a GeoIP database file is in place on the server. The settings page tells you whether it was found. This is a one-off setup, so ask your developer.
